# Lesson 7: Project — Create Your Coaching Practice Blueprint

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this project, you should be able to:
- Synthesise all Module 5 concepts into a coherent coaching practice plan
- Articulate your niche, package, and marketing positioning in clear, client-facing language
- Build actual CoachSheets artifacts (worksheets, programs) that demonstrate technical proficiency
- Evaluate your own readiness to launch or grow your coaching practice

---

## Overview

This project pulls together everything from Module 5 into a single deliverable: your Coaching Practice Blueprint. This is not an academic exercise. By the end of this project, you will have assets you can use immediately — a niche statement, a priced package, a built worksheet, a program structure, and a marketing positioning statement.

Complete all five parts below. Each part has specific deliverables with clear criteria.

---

## Part 1: Define Your Niche (15% of project score)

### Deliverable

Write a one-sentence niche statement using this template:

> "I coach [specific audience] who want [specific outcome] so they can [deeper transformation]."

### Criteria

| Criteria | Meets Standard (Pass) | Exceeds Standard (Distinction) |
|----------|----------------------|-------------------------------|
| Specific audience | Names a demographic, psychographic, or situational group | Narrows further (e.g., "women in tech returning from maternity leave" vs "professionals") |
| Specific outcome | Describes a measurable or observable result | Includes a time dimension or milestone |
| Deeper transformation | Names the emotional or identity-level shift | Connects to a universal human need |

### Examples

- **Pass**: "I coach mid-career professionals who want to transition into purpose-driven work so they can feel fulfilled."
- **Distinction**: "I coach senior engineers in their 30s who are experiencing burnout and want to transition into mission-aligned roles within 6 months so they can reclaim their energy and sense of purpose."

### Part 1 Submission

Your one-sentence niche statement.

## Part 2: Create a Coaching Package (20% of project score)

### Deliverable

Design a complete coaching package with the following elements:

| Element | Required Detail |
|---------|----------------|
| Package name | A memorable name (e.g., "The Clarity Catalyst", "The 90-Day Pivot") |
| Target client | One sentence describing who this is for |
| Duration | Number of weeks or months |
| Number of sessions | Total sessions and frequency |
| Session length | Minutes per session |
| Between-session support | What is included (email, messaging, worksheets) |
| Included worksheets | List specific worksheet types that support the transformation |
| Price | Total price in dollars |
| Payment options | Upfront, monthly instalments, or both |

### Criteria

| Criteria | Meets Standard (Pass) |
|----------|----------------------|
| Completeness | All 9 elements above are present |
| Coherence | The package name, client, duration, and price all feel aligned |
| Price rationale | Price is within market range for your experience level and includes value justification |

### Part 2 Submission

A one-page package proposal document (can be a markdown file or a document) containing all 9 elements.

---

## Part 3: Build an Intake Worksheet in CoachSheets (25% of project score)

### Deliverable

Using CoachSheets, create an intake worksheet designed for your niche client. The worksheet must be a real worksheet in the system (not a mockup or sketch).

### Auto-Verifiable Criteria

These will be checked by the CoachSheets platform:

| Criteria | How It Is Verified |
|----------|-------------------|
| Worksheet exists | A worksheet record exists in the system |
| Worksheet has a category | The category field is populated |
| Worksheet has at least 6 questions | Count of `worksheet_questions` >= 6 |
| Questions use at least 3 different element types | The worksheet uses at least 3 distinct element types (e.g., textarea, scale, cards, checklist) |
| Worksheet is assigned to a client or group | An assignment record links the worksheet to at least one client |
| Assignment was sent (or scheduled) | The assignment has a `send_at` value in the past or future |

### Recommended Questions (adapt to your niche)

1. What brings you to coaching at this time? (textarea)
2. On a scale of 1-10, where are you right now relative to your primary goal? (scale)
3. What have you tried before, and what worked or did not work? (textarea)
4. What does success look like at the end of our engagement? (textarea)
5. What obstacles do you anticipate? (cards — one card per obstacle)
6. What strengths do you bring that will help you succeed? (textarea)
7. How committed are you to this process on a scale of 1-10? (scale)
8. Is there anything else you want me to know? (textarea)

### Part 3 Submission

Provide the CoachSheets worksheet ID(s) and/or a screenshot of the worksheet question list from the platform. Also include a brief note confirming that an assignment was created for at least one client.

## Part 4: Create a 12-Week Program Structure (25% of project score)

### Deliverable

Design a 12-week coaching program structure. If using CoachSheets, create it as a Program with steps. If not using CoachSheets yet, create a detailed markdown table.

### Required Elements

| Week | Theme | Pre-Session Worksheet | In-Session Focus | Post-Session Action |
|------|-------|-----------------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| 1 | (e.g., Foundation) | (worksheet name) | (topic) | (client action) |
| 2 | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| 12 | (e.g., Integration) | (worksheet name) | (topic) | (client action) |

All 12 weeks must be filled in.

### Auto-Verifiable Criteria (if using CoachSheets)

| Criteria | How It Is Verified |
|----------|-------------------|
| Program exists | A program record exists in the system |
| Program has at least 6 steps | Count of `program_steps` >= 6 |
| At least 3 steps have a linked worksheet | Steps reference existing worksheet IDs |
| Program is assigned to at least one client | A program enrollment record exists |

### Self-Assessment Criteria

| Criteria | Reflect On |
|----------|-----------|
| Progression | Does each week build on the previous one? |
| Balance | Is there a mix of reflection, action, and skill-building? |
| Specificity | Are the themes specific to your niche, not generic coaching? |

### Part 4 Submission

A 12-week program table (12 rows in markdown or as a CoachSheets program). If using CoachSheets, provide the program ID.

## Part 5: Marketing Positioning Statement (15% of project score)

### Deliverable

Write a 300-word marketing positioning statement. This is the text that would go on your website's "About" or "Work With Me" page.

### Structure

Your positioning statement should cover:

1. **Who you serve** (1-2 sentences)
2. **The problem they face** (2-3 sentences — make it hurt)
3. **Your approach** (2-3 sentences — what makes you different)
4. **The transformation** (2-3 sentences — what their life looks like after)
5. **A call to action** (1 sentence)

### Criteria

| Criteria | Meets Standard (Pass) |
|----------|----------------------|
| Emotions | Names specific emotional states (frustration, fear, hope, relief) |
| Specificity | References the niche audience and their context |
| Proof | Mentions at least one result, testimonial, or credential |
| Clarity | Could be understood by someone outside the coaching industry |
| Length | Approximately 250-350 words |

### Part 5 Submission

Your marketing positioning statement as plain text or markdown.
