## Answer Key

### Answer 1

**B**. Niching makes your marketing specific, helping ideal clients recognise themselves in your messaging. While a niche can narrow your potential pool (making management easier), the primary benefit is clarity and conversion, not limitation.

### Answer 2

The three circles are:

1. **Your story**: Your lived experience and personal challenges that give you unique insight and credibility.
2. **Your strengths**: Your skills, training, natural talents, and professional background.
3. **Market demand**: Evidence that people are willing to pay for coaching in that area.

A strong niche lives at the intersection of all three.

### Answer 3

**B**. The per-session coach is using time-based pricing (charging per hour of their time). The package coach may be using value-based pricing if the $3,000 price reflects the transformation the client achieves, not just the hours spent.

### Answer 4

Underpricing is dangerous because:
1. It attracts clients who do not value the service and are less likely to do the work, leading to poor outcomes.
2. It leads to coach burnout because you must see many clients to earn a sustainable income.
3. It devalues the coaching profession and makes it harder for all coaches to charge appropriately.

### Answer 5

**B**. According to the onboarding flow, after the signed agreement comes the intake worksheet. The intake is sent before the first session so the coach can review it and prepare.

### Answer 6

1. Connect (build rapport)
2. Explore (understand the client's situation)
3. Reframe (offer a new perspective)
4. Offer (present your package)
5. Decide (ask for the commitment)

### Answer 7

**B**. Your email list is the only audience you own. Social media platforms can change algorithms, suspend accounts, or disappear. Your email list is a direct line you control.

### Answer 8

Two CoachSheets features for automating onboarding:

1. **Magic links**: Send an intake worksheet to a new client via a unique link that requires no account or password. The client clicks, completes, and submits. The coach receives the submission automatically.

2. **Automation rules**: Create a rule triggered by "client created" that automatically sends the intake worksheet. This requires no manual action from the coach — the system sends the worksheet the moment the client is added.

### Answer 9

**B**. Running 1-2 group programs per quarter while maintaining premium 1:1 clients is the most effective hybrid approach. It leverages group coaching for scale while preserving the deep relationships and higher revenue of 1:1 work.

### Answer 10

In a **group coaching program**, the coach leads with a structured curriculum, provides teaching content, facilitates exercises, and coaches individual members within the group setting. The coach is the expert and primary guide.

In a **mastermind**, the group members coach each other. The facilitator's role is to guide the conversation and hold the container, but the primary value comes from peer input and collective wisdom.

A **mastermind** is more suitable for clients who have foundational knowledge and need peer accountability, because they can contribute meaningfully to others while receiving diverse perspectives from the group.
