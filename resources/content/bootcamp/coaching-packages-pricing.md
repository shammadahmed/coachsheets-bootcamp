# Lesson 2: Coaching Packages and Pricing

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:
- Differentiate between session packages and per-session pricing models
- Apply value-based pricing principles rather than time-based pricing
- Design a coaching package with appropriate scope, duration, and deliverables
- Identify common pricing mistakes and how to avoid them

---

## Session Packages vs Per-Session Pricing

One of the first structural decisions you will make as a coach is how to package your services. The two primary models are:

### Per-Session Pricing

You charge a flat fee for each individual session, and the client books one session at a time. This model is simple and low-commitment. It works well for:

- Coaches who are just starting and have no track record
- Clients who are hesitant to commit to a long package
- Very specific, short-term coaching needs (e.g., preparing for a presentation)

The downside is that per-session pricing creates transactional relationships. Clients may cancel or reschedule frequently. You have less predictable income. And because each session is evaluated individually, a "bad" session feels like wasted money to the client.

### Session Packages

A package bundles multiple sessions together at a single price, often with additional resources (worksheets, email support, assessments). This model is the industry standard for professional coaching and is strongly recommended by the ICF. Benefits include:

- **Predictable revenue**: You know your income for the next 3-6 months.
- **Client commitment**: Clients who have paid for a package are more engaged and less likely to quit.
- **Better outcomes**: Real transformation takes multiple sessions over time. Packages align the incentive structure with actual results.
- **Simpler operations**: You schedule recurring sessions rather than renegotiating each time.

Most experienced coaches use packages exclusively. Per-session pricing is primarily used for introductory "sample" sessions or very narrow engagements.

## Value-Based Pricing vs Time-Based Pricing

The single biggest pricing mistake coaches make is charging based on time. "I will charge $100 per hour" sounds reasonable, but it fundamentally misunderstands what you are selling.

### Time-Based Pricing

Time-based pricing treats coaching as a commodity. You are selling an hour of your presence. This model:

- Caps your income at the number of hours you can work
- Frames coaching as a cost rather than an investment
- Encourages you to watch the clock instead of focusing on outcomes
- Makes it harder to raise prices — you are just an "expensive hourly worker"

### Value-Based Pricing

Value-based prices what the client gains, not what you give. A client who lands a $50,000 raise, saves a failing marriage, or finally launches their business is not buying an hour of conversation. They are buying a life-changing outcome. Your price should reflect the value of that outcome.

For example, if an executive coaching client earns $200,000/year and you help them perform at a level that earns a $50,000 promotion, the value of your coaching is at least $50,000. Charging $3,000 for a 3-month engagement is a bargain for the client relative to the outcome.

To shift to value-based pricing:

1. **Quantify the outcome**: What is the financial or emotional value of the transformation you deliver?
2. **Price below that value**: Your price should feel like an obvious investment.
3. **Communicate in value terms**: "This program is designed to help you achieve X, which is worth Y. My fee is a fraction of that."

You do not need to justify your price by the hour. You need to justify it by the result.

## Common Package Structures

Here are three proven package structures that coaches use. Each aligns with a different type of transformation.

### 3-Month Intensive (12 sessions)

Best for: Clients with a clear, urgent goal who want fast progress.

| Component | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Duration | 12 weeks (3 months) |
| Sessions | 12 x 45-50 minute sessions (weekly) |
| Between sessions | Unlimited Voxer/WhatsApp support |
| Worksheets | Weekly worksheet delivered before each session |
| Price range | $1,500 - $6,000 |

### 6-Month Engagement (12 sessions, bi-weekly)

Best for: Deeper transformation work, habit change, identity shifts.

| Component | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Duration | 24 weeks (6 months) |
| Sessions | 12 x 50 minute sessions (bi-weekly) |
| Between sessions | Email support, monthly check-in worksheet |
| Assessments | Intake assessment and midpoint progress review |
| Price range | $2,400 - $9,000 |

### 12-Session Package (flexible cadence)

Best for: Clients who travel frequently or have unpredictable schedules.

| Component | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Duration | Up to 6 months to use all sessions |
| Sessions | 12 x 50 minutes (booked on demand) |
| Between sessions | Email support |
| Bonus | Recorded guided worksheets included |
| Price range | $1,800 - $7,200 |

## What to Include in Your Package

Beyond sessions, your package should include elements that support the client's transformation between sessions. This is where a platform like CoachSheets becomes invaluable.

- **Intake worksheet**: Gather client history, goals, and baseline assessment before the first session.
- **Between-session worksheets**: Keep clients engaged and reflecting between calls. Send via CoachSheets magic link or automation rules.
- **Session recap notes**: Summarise key insights and action items after each session.
- **Resource library**: Curated articles, exercises, or recordings relevant to your niche.
- **Email or messaging support**: Define boundaries (e.g., 24-hour response, business hours only).
- **Progress assessments**: Mid-point and end-point evaluations to demonstrate ROI.

The more structured your package, the more professional you appear and the better your client outcomes will be.

## Pricing Research and Positioning

The coaching market spans a wide range of price points. Here are typical ranges based on experience level:

| Experience Level | Per Session | Monthly Retainer | 3-Month Package |
|-----------------|-------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| New coach (0-2 years) | $75 - $150 | $300 - $600 | $900 - $1,800 |
| Established coach (2-5 years) | $150 - $250 | $600 - $1,000 | $1,800 - $3,000 |
| Senior coach (5+ years) | $250 - $500 | $1,000 - $2,000 | $3,000 - $6,000 |
| Premium/Executive coach | $500 - $1,500+ | $2,000 - $6,000+ | $6,000 - $18,000+ |

**Pricing warning**: Do not underprice. Charging $30-50 per session is not a strategy for building a practice; it is a strategy for burnout. Low prices attract clients who do not value coaching and are unlikely to do the work. The clients who benefit most from coaching are those who invest significantly in it.

Raise your prices as you gain confidence, testimonials, and results. Your price should rise faster than your experience.
