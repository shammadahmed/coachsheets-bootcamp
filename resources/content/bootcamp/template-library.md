## Introduction

You don't need to invent everything from scratch. The CoachSheets Template Library contains dozens of battle-tested frameworks used by top coaches worldwide.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:
- Navigate the CoachSheets Template Library.
- Clone a public template into your private workspace.
- Customize a cloned template to match your brand voice.

## The Power of Templates

Why build a "Wheel of Life" worksheet from scratch when you can use ours? Our library includes everything from Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) inspired thought records to simple daily habit trackers.

By starting with a template, you save hours of formatting and can immediately begin customizing the questions to fit your specific coaching niche.
