# Lesson 6.7: Project — Specialization Deep Dive

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of this project, you should be able to:
- Select a coaching specialization aligned with your interests, experience, and market demand
- Research the unique needs, challenges, and evidence base for that population
- Adapt coaching frameworks and communication styles for a specific niche
- Design a 6-week coaching programme tailored to your specialization using CoachSheets
- Articulate why this specialization suits your coaching identity

---

## Why Specialize?

Generalist coaching is a valid and common path, but specialization offers distinct advantages:

- **Expertise:** You develop deep knowledge of a specific population's needs and challenges.
- **Credibility:** Clients trust a coach who understands their unique context (e.g., "she specialises in ADHD coaching" carries more weight than "she is a life coach").
- **Marketing clarity:** It is easier to reach a specific audience than "everyone."
- **Higher rates:** Specialised coaches often command higher fees because they solve specific, recognised problems.
- **Deeper work:** When you coach the same type of client repeatedly, you recognise patterns faster and go deeper sooner.

> "The riches are in the niches." — Common coaching industry adage

However, specialization does not mean you can never coach anyone else. Many coaches have a primary niche and work with other clients outside it. Specialization is about depth in one area, not limitation in all others.

---

## Choosing Your Specialization

Consider the intersection of three factors:

1. **Your passion and experience** — What populations or issues do you care about? What life experience do you bring?
2. **Market demand** — Who is willing to pay for coaching in this area? Is the market saturated or growing?
3. **Your natural strengths** — What kind of coaching do you do best? Directive or non-directive? Emotional or strategic?

## Project Structure

This project has two deliverables:

### Deliverable 1: 1000-Word Specialization Deep Dive

Write a 1000-word (+/- 100 words) analysis of how you would adapt your coaching for a specific population. Structure it as follows:

**Section 1: The Population and Their Needs (approx. 250 words)**
- Who is this population? What is the typical context they are in?
- What are their most common coaching goals and challenges?
- What distinguishes them from a general coaching client?

**Section 2: Evidence and Research (approx. 200 words)**
- What does the research say about effective support for this population?
- Cite at least two credible sources (peer-reviewed research, recognised books, professional associations).
- What common misconceptions should a coach working with this population avoid?

**Section 3: Adapted Coaching Approach (approx. 300 words)**
- How would you adapt your coaching communication style for this population?
- What frameworks or tools are especially relevant (or irrelevant)?
- What specific coaching competencies do you need to develop to serve this population well?
- What ethical considerations or boundaries apply?

**Section 4: The Coach's Fit (approx. 150 words)**
- Why are you well-suited to this specialization? (life experience, training, natural strengths)
- What gaps would you need to fill (training, supervision, personal work)?
- What is your long-term vision for this specialization?

### Deliverable 2: 6-Week CoachSheets Programme

Using the CoachSheets platform, build a 6-week coaching programme tailored to your specialization.

**Requirements:**

| Element | Minimum Requirement |
|---|---|
| Programme title and description | A clear programme name and a 100-200 word description of what the programme offers |
| 6 weekly session themes | Each week has a named theme with a 50-100 word description |
| At least 6 worksheets | Worksheets with questions and elements appropriate to the specialization |
| At least 1 automation | An automation rule (e.g., send a check-in worksheet 2 days after each session) |
| At least 1 recurring schedule | A recurring schedule for ongoing support (e.g., weekly reflection prompts) |
| Client intake worksheet | An intake worksheet with at least 8 questions specific to this population |

**Worksheet Ideas by Specialization:**

| Specialization | Worksheet Ideas |
|---|---|
| Career | Values card sort, skills audit, ideal career day, networking plan, gap analysis |
| Wellness | Habit tracker, energy audit, sleep log, triggers and cues, support system map |
| ADHD | Executive function self-assessment, time blindness log, focus environment audit, body doubling plan |
| Financial | Money autobiography, spending values, debt snowball plan, financial vision board |
| Relationship | Communication patterns inventory, conflict style assessment, relationship vision, boundaries worksheet |
| Spiritual | Spiritual autobiography, values clarification, purpose statement, gratitude practice |
| Executive | Leadership strengths assessment, 360-degree feedback reflection, decision audit, delegation map |

## Rubric

| Criteria | Excellent (4 pts) | Proficient (3 pts) | Developing (2 pts) | Needs Work (1 pt) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Depth Dive: Population Understanding** | Rich, nuanced understanding of the population; specific needs articulated clearly | Good understanding; most key needs identified | General understanding; some key needs missed | Superficial; generic descriptions |
| **Depth Dive: Research Quality** | 2+ strong sources cited correctly; research meaningfully integrated | 2 sources cited; some integration | 1 source cited; minimal integration | No sources cited; no research foundation |
| **Depth Dive: Adaptation Specificity** | Concrete, specific adaptations to coaching approach; detailed examples | Clear adaptations described; some examples | General adaptations; few examples | No real adaptation; generic coaching approach |
| **Programme Design: Structure** | Clear, logical 6-week progression; each week builds on previous | Good structure; most weeks connect logically | Some structure; weeks feel disconnected | No clear progression; random topics |
| **Programme Design: Worksheets** | 6+ worksheets; highly relevant to specialization; creative | 6 worksheets; relevant to specialization | Fewer than 6 worksheets; partially relevant | Few or no worksheets; not specialization-specific |
| **Programme Design: Automation & Scheduling** | Automation + recurring schedule both present; thoughtfully configured | Both present; basic configuration | One of two present | Neither present |
| **Intake Worksheet** | 8+ questions; highly specific to population; assessment quality | 8 questions; specific to population | Fewer than 8 questions; generic | No intake worksheet or generic questions |
| **Self-Reflection** | Honest assessment of fit; clear growth plan; compelling vision | Good self-assessment; some growth plan | Surface-level reflection; vague plan | No reflection on personal fit |

**Total possible: 32 points**

## Assignment

1. Choose your specialization. Write a brief paragraph (100 words) explaining why you chose it.

2. Research your specialization. Find at least two credible sources (peer-reviewed research, authoritative books, recognised professional associations). Write a 200-word annotated bibliography for each source summarising the key findings and their relevance to coaching.

3. Complete Deliverable 1: Write the 1000-word Specialization Deep Dive following the structure above.

4. Complete Deliverable 2: Build the 6-week CoachSheets programme, including all worksheets, automation, and the intake worksheet.

5. Add your specialization programme to your CoachSheets library with appropriate tags and a public description.

6. Submit both deliverables along with a link to your programme on CoachSheets.
