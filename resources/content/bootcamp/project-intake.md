## Introduction

It's time to put everything you've learned into practice. In this project, you will build a complete, automated Client Intake Flow using the CoachSheets platform.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this project, you should be able to:
- Create a multi-step digital intake process.
- Automate the assignment of worksheets to new clients.
- Review client submissions in your dashboard.

## Project Specifications

Your intake flow must meet the following criteria:

1. **Intake Form**: Create a new worksheet titled "Client Intake". It must include fields for their name, primary goal, and biggest current obstacle.
2. **Automated Assignment**: Set up a rule so that when a client completes the Intake Form, they are automatically assigned the "Wheel of Life" template from the library.
3. **Branding**: Customize the forms with your specific coaching colors and logo.

## Submission Guidelines

Once you have built your flow, test it by creating a dummy client account and walking through the process yourself. Experiencing the flow from your client's perspective is the best way to catch friction points.

Good luck!
