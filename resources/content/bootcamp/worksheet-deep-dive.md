Worksheets are the tactical tools you use to execute your coaching frameworks. A great worksheet gathers data asynchronously, provokes deep reflection, and holds the client accountable. Below are 10 of the most powerful worksheets you can implement in your practice today.

---

### 1. The Wheel of Life
**Best for:** Initial Intake & Quarterly Reviews
**Concept:** Clients rate their satisfaction (1-10) across 8 core life domains (Career, Finance, Health, Family, Romance, Personal Growth, Fun, Physical Environment).
**Digital Advantage:** CoachSheets can automatically graph these inputs into a radar chart, instantly visualizing areas of imbalance.

### 2. CBT Thought Record (Coaching Adaptation)
**Best for:** Overcoming Imposter Syndrome & Anxiety
**Concept:** A structured grid where clients log an activating event, their automatic negative thought, the emotional intensity, evidence *for* the thought, and evidence *against* the thought.
**Digital Advantage:** Clients can log this on their phones in real-time as the event happens, rather than trying to remember it days later during a session.

### 3. Daily Habit Tracker
**Best for:** Execution & Accountability
**Concept:** A simple daily checklist of 3-5 core habits the client has committed to.
**Digital Advantage:** CoachSheets can aggregate this data to show weekly compliance percentages, preventing clients from relying on biased memory.

### 4. SMART Goal Setter
**Best for:** Project Planning
**Concept:** Forces the client to break down a vague desire into a Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound goal.

### 5. Limiting Beliefs Reframer
**Best for:** Breaking through plateaus
**Concept:** Two columns: "The Story I Tell Myself" and "The Objective Truth." This exercise separates subjective narratives from factual reality.

### 6. Core Values Assessment
**Best for:** Career Transition & Life Planning
**Concept:** A multi-select exercise where clients narrow down a list of 50 values to their top 5 non-negotiables.

### 7. Time Audit Log
**Best for:** Time Management & Burnout
**Concept:** Clients log their activities in 30-minute increments for 3 days to identify "time vampires" and energy drains.
**Digital Advantage:** Much easier to do on a mobile device than carrying around a paper ledger.

### 8. Eisenhower Priority Matrix
**Best for:** Overwhelm & Prioritization
**Concept:** A 2x2 grid categorizing tasks by Urgency and Importance (Do, Decide, Delegate, Delete).

### 9. Fear-Setting Exercise
**Best for:** Major life decisions (Adapted from Tim Ferriss)
**Concept:** Instead of defining goals, the client defines their worst-case scenarios, the steps they could take to prevent them, and the steps to repair them if they happen.

### 10. Weekly Reflection Journal
**Best for:** Ongoing retainers
**Concept:** A 3-question Friday wrap-up: 
1. What was your biggest win? 
2. What was your biggest challenge? 
3. What is the #1 priority for next week?
**Digital Advantage:** Provides a perfect agenda for Monday's live coaching session.
