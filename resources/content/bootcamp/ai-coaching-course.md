## Introduction

Artificial Intelligence is the most powerful tool to enter the coaching space since the internet. However, as a coach, you must navigate its ethical implications carefully.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:
- Differentiate between safe AI summarization and dangerous AI diagnostics.
- Write effective prompts for analyzing client intake forms.
- Configure CoachSheets AI to adhere to coaching ethics.

## The Ethical Boundaries

AI should **never** be used to diagnose, treat, or offer clinical therapy to a client. CoachSheets is strictly a non-clinical tool. 

Instead, AI should act as your incredibly fast assistant. It can read a 5,000-word journal entry from your client and instantly extract the three main themes they are struggling with, so you can spend your 60-minute session addressing exactly what matters.
