## Introduction

The life coaching industry is undergoing a massive transformation. For decades, the standard model has been the "one hour per week" video call or in-person session, supported by PDFs emailed back and forth. 

As a modern digital practitioner, your goal is to break free from this synchronous trap. 

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:
- Identify the core bottlenecks of traditional coaching methods.
- Explain the benefits of asynchronous client check-ins.
- Articulate how digitizing worksheets enhances accountability.

## The Paradigm Shift

Most coaches spend 80% of their time on administrative tasks, finding documents, or catching up with the client during the first 20 minutes of a session. 

By utilizing dynamic, digital worksheets (like those provided in the CoachSheets library), you can gather all of a client's reflections *asynchronously*. When you hop on a call, you already know exactly what they struggled with during the week. This allows you to dive straight into deep, transformative work.
