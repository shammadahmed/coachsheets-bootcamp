## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this ebook, you should be able to:
- Map your existing PDF worksheets to interactive digital formats.
- Understand the data security requirements for storing client answers.
- Implement automated nudges to improve client completion rates.

## Sneak Peek

Digitizing your work isn't just about moving from paper to pixels; it's about unlocking data. When a client fills out a paper worksheet, the data is trapped. When they fill out a digital worksheet, the data can be visualized, tracked over time, and even summarized by AI before your session.

## Assignment

1. Gather your top 5 most used coaching PDFs.
2. Identify which fields are repetitive and could be automated.
